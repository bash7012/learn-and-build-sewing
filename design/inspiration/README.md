# Design inspiration

Screenshots of other people's sewing sites, kept as a reference for layout and
rhythm only. They are third-party trade dress: they live here, never under
`public/`, and are not published with the site. Do not copy their copy, their
photographs, or their logotypes.

## The direction taken from them

The two references that set the landing page are
`the-fold-line-hero-orange.png` and `brindille-and-twig-landing-categories.png`.
What they share:

- **A full-bleed hero photograph.** Edge to edge, roughly two thirds of the
  first screen, with the text sitting on the calm part of the image rather than
  in a band above it. A short kicker, a large title, one sentence, then buttons.
- **A centred statement below the fold line.** One wide-tracked uppercase
  heading that says plainly what the site is, and one short centred paragraph
  under it. No sidebar-width column of prose as the first thing you read.
- **Category cards, not a list.** A four-up (or three-up) row where each card is
  an image, an uppercase title, a small bold qualifier — Brindille uses the size
  range, `PREEMIE - 2T` — and two or three lines of description.
- **Sans-serif, wide letter-spacing, uppercase for anything structural.**
  Headings are quiet and tracked out; the emphasis comes from scale and
  whitespace, not weight or colour.
- **Generous vertical rhythm.** Sections are separated by space, not by rules
  and boxes.

## What is deliberately not taken

- No commerce furniture: no prices, star ratings, wishlist hearts, cart, or
  "quick view".
- No stacked promotional bars, carousels, or newsletter interruptions.
- No client-side JavaScript for any of it, per `CONSTITUTION.md`.
- Card qualifiers state real status (`draft`, `in-progress`, `complete`), not
  invented skill levels or size ranges.
- Where a page has no photograph yet, the card shows a drafting-paper
  placeholder rather than a stock photograph standing in for work that has not
  been sewn.
