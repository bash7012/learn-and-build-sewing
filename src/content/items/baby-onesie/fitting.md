Applies to both the [self-drafted](/items/baby-onesie/self-drafted) and [graded pattern](/items/baby-onesie/graded-pattern) branches. Start from the [toile](/items/baby-onesie/toile).

## Fit checklist

At rest and in motion:

- [ ] Neck
- [ ] Shoulder
- [ ] Chest
- [ ] Crotch
- [ ] Leg
- [ ] Dressing over the head or with a placket
- [ ] Note anything that pulls, gapes, or twists

## Diapering and movement

- [ ] Can a diaper go on cleanly?
- [ ] Can the child crawl or kick without the onesie riding up?
- [ ] Can the child crawl or kick without the onesie binding?
- [ ] Note any place fabric stretch is doing the work that ease should do

## Adjustments to make

- [ ] What to pin
- [ ] What to recut
- [ ] What to treat as fabric stretch rather than a pattern error
- [ ] Write each surviving change clearly before leaving the fitting session

## What carries back to the pattern

Transfer changes to **both** branches so the next size or the next onesie is not a fresh guess:

- [ ] Update [Self-drafted](/items/baby-onesie/self-drafted)
- [ ] Update [Graded pattern](/items/baby-onesie/graded-pattern)
- [ ] Note anything that also belongs on [Using the Block](/foundations/using-the-block)

## Photo log

- [ ] Pin or fit details (no face)
- [ ] Before and after an adjustment on paper
